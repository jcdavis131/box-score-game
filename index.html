How to play

Draft. Each round you're dealt a hand from one shared 108-card deck. Pick a card to keep, then pass the rest. Hands shrink until the round ends.

Score the round. Alley-Oop pairs score 5 each. Triple-Double sets of 3 score 10 each. Buckets score 1 / 3 / 6 / 10 / 15 for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+ of a kind. All-Star Votes rank by total stars: most 6, second 3, ties split. Free Throw 1, Layup 2, Deep Three 3.

Hot Hand triples your next shot in the same round (3 / 6 / 9) and is worth 0 if no shot follows. Timeout lets you draft a second card that turn — chain them and keep drafting.

Rings are the only cards kept all game. At the end: most rings +6, fewest −6, ties split; if everyone ties, no swing.

Hands. 2 players get 10 cards, 3 get 9, 4 get 6, 5 get 5 — per round. Solo is you against Marv the Collector, Dex the Gunner, and Prudence the Ledger.

Unofficial fan game. Player and coach names appear as text references only — no photos, no team logos, no league affiliation. Same deck code means the same shuffled deck.
